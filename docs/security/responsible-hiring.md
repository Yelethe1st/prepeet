# Responsible Hiring

**Status:** Required principles; jurisdiction-specific approval pending  
**Owner:** Responsible-AI, legal, product, and security governance  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-23

## Product boundary

Prepeet provides structured evidence to a human reviewer. It does not make, automate, or conceal an employment decision.

## Required safeguards

- Named human reviewer and reasoned decision.
- Evidence and sufficiency visible for material conclusions.
- Unknown/insufficient/unassessable separated from poor.
- No auto-advance or auto-reject.
- No personality, emotion, honesty, culture-fit, health, protected-class, or future-performance inference.
- Practice data excluded from employer screening.
- Accent never scored.
- Articulation excluded from employer scoring by default.
- Unverified claims and contradictions described neutrally.
- Job requirements shown as evidence checklist, not match percentage.
- Overrides, access, exports, and decisions audited.
- Re-review/appeal and incident/accommodation architecture operational before launch.

## Candidate transparency

Before screening, disclose employer, purpose, AI involvement, recording/processing, access, retention, rights route, accommodations, result disclosure, and human decision ownership. Consent is versioned and not bundled with optional model improvement.

## Articulation and communication

A job-related communication criterion may enter screening only after relevance evidence, candidate disclosure, validated measurement, accessibility/bias testing, legal/product approval, accommodation, assessability handling, and reviewer-visible evidence. Vocal affect and response latency are excluded.

## Comparison

Off by default. If approved: same role/comparable rubric, two to four candidates, coverage and uncertainty, no ranking, explicit indistinguishable outcomes, and mandatory individual evidence inspection.

## Fairness monitoring

Monitor transcription/assessability quality, insufficiency, interruption, accommodation completion, overrides, re-review, and outcome patterns. Do not collect demographic attributes casually; define lawful purpose, governance, access, retention, and analysis before collection.

## Evaluation governance

Model/rubric/prompt publication requires representative datasets, evidence grounding, unsupported-fact measurement, stability, language/accent/device/accessibility testing, human review, cost/latency, approvers, monitoring, and rollback/freeze.

## Appeals and correction

Support candidate/system request, eligibility, original input freeze, assigned/independent reviewer, SLA, outcome/rationale, disclosure, and append-only history. Whether it is a legal right or platform policy must be resolved per launch jurisdiction.

## Stop-ship

Practice/screen leakage, uncited material conclusions, accent/protected/personality inference, hidden automatic decision, inaccessible core path without alternative, unresolved legal disclosure, untested tenant isolation, or inability to freeze an integrity incident.

## Launch evidence

Legal/privacy review, impact assessment where required, supported language/accent limits, accommodations, human review/appeal policy, evaluation quality report, independent security test, provider terms, candidate disclosure, monitoring, and incident/re-review exercise.
