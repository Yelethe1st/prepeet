What a re-run can and cannot do

A re-run evaluates the same transcript against the current rubric version. It cannot recover audio that was never captured, and it frequently produces the same band. Where the complaint is about the recording rather than the reading of it, a new interview is the honest answer.

Why upholding needs writing

A candidate who appeals is owed an explanation whichever way it goes. Northwind requires a written rationale on every upheld decision; it is sent to the candidate, kept for 18 months and sampled at calibration to check that upholding is not becoming a reflex.
