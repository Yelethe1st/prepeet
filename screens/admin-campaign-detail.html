    What the candidate is told

    Northwind standard disclosure v3 · shown before consent, and again on the invitation page.

    Who is asking. Northwind Health System has invited you to a screening interview for Registered Nurse — Intensive Care.

    How it works. The interview is conducted by an AI interviewer and lasts about 40 minutes. It is recorded, transcribed and evaluated against a published rubric.

    Who sees it. Named reviewers at Northwind Health System. Every time someone opens your transcript or recording, it is logged.

    What you will be told. That your interview was submitted. Northwind decides what to share beyond that, and a person — not Prepeet — makes the hiring decision.

    How long it is kept. 18 months, unless you ask for it sooner or a legal hold applies.

    What you can ask for. Captions, push-to-talk, extra thinking time, or a different way to take part if voice is not accessible to you. None of these change how you are assessed.

    Pause this campaign

    What pausing does. No new invitations can be issued. The five candidates who already hold a link can still complete their interview, and the three awaiting review stay reviewable.
