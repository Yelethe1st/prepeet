Why an invitation needs a campaign

Comparing two candidates only means something if they were asked comparable questions and scored against the same published rubric. The campaign is where that is fixed. Publishing a new rubric version creates a new version — it never rewrites a campaign that has already issued invitations, and it never re-scores an interview a candidate has already sat.

What a campaign does not do
  • It does not rank, shortlist or reject. Every decision on a campaign is recorded against a named reviewer.
  • It does not carry a candidate's practice history. Practice is candidate-owned and never attached to a screening.
  • It does not change once invitations are out. Editing an open campaign creates a new version for future invitations only.
