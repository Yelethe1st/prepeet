What an integration actually does

An applicant tracking connection keeps candidate stages in step: when somebody completes a screening here, their stage moves there. A webhook is the raw version of the same thing — Prepeet posts a signed JSON event to a URL you own, and your systems decide what to do with it. Neither sends anything to a third party that your own staff could not already see in this console.

Disconnect this integration?

Disconnecting stops all data flowing between Prepeet and this system. Nothing already in Prepeet is deleted.

  • Candidates, evaluations and decisions stay exactly as they are here.
  • Stage changes stop reaching the other system, so your recruiters will have to update it by hand.
  • Reconnecting later needs a fresh authorisation and re-mapping of stages.
