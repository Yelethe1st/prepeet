Interview configuration

How the conversation is run. Every candidate invited from this screen gets the same configuration.

Candidates can finish early. The interview stops at the target duration.

Fewer questions means less evidence per competency, and lower confidence in the summary.

Captions

Captions are on by default. Candidates can also turn them on themselves at any point.

Interviewer persona

Personas are interview styles, not assessments of candidates. The persona changes how a question is asked and followed up — it never changes the rubric, the competencies or how an answer is evidenced.

Deadline and reminders

Give people enough room to find a quiet 40 minutes. Two weeks is the default.

Closes at 23:59 in each candidate's own timezone, not yours. Northwind Health System is set to Europe/Berlin.

Reminder schedule

Insert a variable

Plain English works best. Candidates who understand what the interview is for finish it far more often.

Accessibility and accommodations

Recorded on the invitation so a reviewer reading the evaluation knows the conditions the interview was taken under.

Accommodations do not affect evaluation. Extra time, captions, transcripts and pauses change how the interview is taken, never how the answers are scored against the rubric, and they are not shown as a flag on the candidate's evaluation.

What the candidate is told

Sent with every invitation. This block cannot be edited or switched off.

Always included
What Northwind receives
A recording and transcript of the answers, plus a competency summary that quotes what the candidate actually said and says where the evidence was thin.
What the candidate sees
Confirmation that the interview was submitted. No score, no band, no ranking and no recommendation.
Who decides
A named person on the Northwind hiring team. Prepeet does not decide the outcome.
What is never assessed
Personality, accent, appearance, emotion or how confident somebody sounds. Only what was said, against the published rubric.
Candidate rights
Request a human re-review, request a copy of their data, or withdraw at any time using the link in the email.
Retention
18 months from the interview date, then deleted.
