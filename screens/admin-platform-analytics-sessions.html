Completion rate and median duration, last 30 days.

Read these as format effects, not candidate effects. A panel simulation runs 44 minutes with eight questions and a sceptical chair; a recruiter screen runs 15 minutes with four. Lower completion on the longer formats reflects how much time and stamina the format demands. Nothing here says anything about the ability of the candidates who chose or were assigned each format.
