Throttling policy

Applies to every plan; per-tenant values are set in the table above

Soft limit — warn

Default 80% of the monthly allowance. The tenant owner and every tenant admin receive an email and an in-app banner showing consumption, the projected hard-limit date and how to request more. Nothing is blocked. Recruiters and candidates see no change.

Re-notified every 5 percentage points after the first crossing, at most once per day.

Hard limit — block new starts

Default 100% of the allowance. New interviews cannot be started for that tenant. Interviews already in progress always complete, are transcribed, evaluated and delivered — we never cut a candidate off mid-answer. Scheduled invitations stay valid and become startable again as soon as headroom exists.

Platform admins can grant temporary headroom without changing the plan.

Spend cap — stop overage billing

Only applies to tenants whose overage policy is “allow with overage billing”. Once billed overage reaches the cap, the tenant behaves as if it hit a hard limit rather than continuing to accrue charges. This protects the customer from a surprise invoice.

A cap is never applied silently — the owner is notified at 80% of the cap.

Grant temporary headroom

A grant adds interview minutes on top of the tenant's allowance for a fixed period. It does not change the plan, the invoice or the tenant's own settings.

Roughly 200 interviews at the 30-minute platform average.

Enter at least 500 minutes.

Unused granted minutes are discarded at expiry; they never roll into the next month.

Be specific — this is read by whoever reviews the account next month.
