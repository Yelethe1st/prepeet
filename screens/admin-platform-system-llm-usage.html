Text summary: audio processing (realtime voice plus transcription) accounts for $6,559.37, or 68.2% of the $9,618.69 total. Text processing (evaluation plus embeddings) accounts for $3,059.32, or 31.8%.

Why audio dominates

Realtime voice is billed per minute of the conversation, and it runs for the whole interview — a 40-minute panel costs the same whether the candidate says a lot or a little. Evaluation runs once at the end over a transcript of a few thousand tokens. So spend scales with interview minutes, not with how much analysis we do. The two levers that actually move the number are shorter default interview lengths and routing short sessions to the cheaper transcription tier.
