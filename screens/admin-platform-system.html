Retry this job?

Re-enqueues job at the head of its queue using the original idempotency key, so a duplicate evaluation cannot be produced.

The retry is recorded in the audit log against your account.

Retry all 6 failed jobs?

Two of the six are marked non-retryable (rubric_version_mismatch, tls_handshake_failure) and will be skipped unless you fix the cause first.

Retrying 4 jobs adds roughly 90 seconds of work to the evaluation queue, which is already above its objective.

Send to dead-letter?

Moves job out of the live queue into the dead-letter store. It will not be retried automatically and the affected tenant will not receive the result until someone replays it.

Dead-letter all 6 jobs?

Six tenants stop receiving these results until someone replays them by hand. Prefer retrying the four retryable jobs first.

Affected tenants: Northwind Health System (2), Orbital Labs, Meridian Schools Trust, Caldera Capital, Brightpath Commercial.
