How to read this comparison

  • Each score is a summary of what a candidate said in one conversation, matched against the Northwind Health System rubric for this role. It is not a measure of the person, and it does not predict how they will perform in the unit.
  • Differences inside overlapping confidence ranges are not meaningful. If two ranges touch, the evidence cannot tell you which candidate is stronger — treat them as level and read the quotes instead.
  • “Insufficient evidence” is not a low score. It means the conversation never reached that competency. Nothing can be concluded from it in either direction.
  • Prepeet does not rank candidates and does not recommend a hire. There is no ordering on this page. The decision, and the reason for it, belongs to a named reviewer.
