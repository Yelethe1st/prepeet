Competency signal is a summary of what a candidate said in one recorded conversation, scored against the Northwind Health System rubric. It is not a prediction of job performance, and it says nothing about personality or honesty. Where the conversation did not produce enough evidence, no score is shown at all — and that row is never sorted into a rank position.

Review calibration Edit the rubric Scores drifting from what your reviewers conclude? Calibration shows where.

Rubric and calibration settings belong to tenant administrators. If a score looks wrong, use “Request re-review” on the row and say what the evaluation missed.
