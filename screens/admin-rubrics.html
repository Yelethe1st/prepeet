Starter templates

Three starting points, written by our assessment team with practising panellists. None of them is validated for your organisation — treat every anchor as a draft until your own panel has read it.

Clinical panel4 competencies

Clinical panel

Built for structured clinical interviews: clinical reasoning, patient safety, escalation and handover, compassionate communication. Anchors expect the candidate to name observations and sequence. Written against UK NHS-style panel practice — if you hire to different escalation routes, the Escalation anchors will need rewriting first.

Commercial4 competencies

Commercial

For analytical and customer-facing commercial roles: discovery or analysis, judgement under constraint, stakeholder work, and discipline in reporting. The anchors reward candidates who can name figures, so they under-serve people moving in from a domain where those figures were not theirs to see.

Technical4 competencies

Technical

For engineering roles assessed by conversation rather than code: design, diagnosis, technical communication, collaboration. It assumes the interview is verbal — it will not tell you whether somebody can write the code, and it should not be used as though it does.

Before you publish a rubric

Have somebody who does the job read the anchors. An anchor that only a recruiter can picture will be applied inconsistently, and inconsistency is where adverse impact starts.
