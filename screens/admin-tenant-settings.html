From Northwind Health System <no-reply@prepeet.example> To amara.osei@example.com Subject Your interview for Registered Nurse — ICU at Northwind Health System
Northwind Health System

Hello Amara,

Thank you for applying to our intensive care team. The next step is a 25-minute recorded interview you can take whenever suits you, on a phone or a laptop.

You will speak with an AI interviewer called Lena, who will ask eight questions about your clinical practice. There is no camera — audio only.

Start your interview

Please complete it by Sunday 6 September 2026.

Northwind Health System receives a written evaluation of your answers, the full transcript, and the audio recording. The evaluation quotes what you said as evidence. A person at Northwind Health System reads it and makes the decision — Prepeet does not decide anything about your application.

If you need an adjustment — extra time, a screen reader, captions, a different format, or a live conversation instead — reply to this email or contact accessibility@northwindhealth.example. Asking for an adjustment is not recorded on your application and is never shared with the panel as part of your evaluation.

You can withdraw at any point and ask for your recording to be deleted.

What candidates are told the employer receives

Shown in the invitation email, on the consent screen before the interview starts, and again on the confirmation screen afterwards. Keep it accurate — this is the basis on which the candidate consents.

It must state what is shared and who decides. Prepeet blocks publishing text that claims the system measures personality, emotion or honesty.

This text matches what is actually shared. Reviewers at Northwind Health System see the evaluation, transcript, evidence spans and audio. They do not see practice sessions, and candidates are never shown a screening score.

Accessibility and adjustments

Deadlines and reminders

Reminders are never sent between 20:00 and 08:00 in the candidate's own timezone.

Why this matters

A recorded interview is an unusual thing to ask of someone. The more precisely you describe what happens to the recording and who decides, the fewer people drop out — and the fewer complaints you handle later.
