Why Prepeet links expire

  • A short window limits the damage of a leaked email. Forwarded messages, shared mailboxes and printed handovers are all common in the workplaces we serve. An hour-old link is a much smaller problem than a permanent one.
  • Each link works exactly once. Requesting a new link invalidates the previous one immediately, so only the newest email in your inbox is ever live.
  • Mail scanners sometimes open the link first. Many hospital and school gateways visit every URL before delivery. That consumes the single use and lands you here through no fault of your own.
