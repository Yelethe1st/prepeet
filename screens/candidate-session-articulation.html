Why there is nothing here

How you sound is not scored for an employer. Northwind Health System receives evidence about what you said — your reasoning, your examples, the competencies the conversation reached — and nothing about your pace, your pauses or your accent.

That is a deliberate boundary rather than a setting. A communication criterion could only enter a screening interview after it was shown to be job-related, disclosed to you, tested for accessibility and bias, and approved — and even then it would be evidence a person reads, not a delivery score.

Two things to change next

Not ten. Two.

State the decision before the background

In question 1 your main answer arrived 38 seconds in, after three sentences of context. A listener who is deciding whether to follow you has usually decided by then. Your reasoning was sound — it simply arrived after the point at which it was being judged.

A shape that would have worked

  1. Lead with the decision — “I would separate the availability read path from the reservation write path.”
  2. One sentence of context — why the load shape forces it.
  3. Your reasoning.
  4. Close with the result you saw when you did it.

This is your own sentence reordered. We have not added a fact, a metric or an outcome you did not say.

Let the pause do the work the filler is doing

4.8 fillers per 100 words, clustered almost entirely at the start of answers — you are thinking out loud while you decide where to begin. A one-second silence reads as consideration. “So, um, I think what I would probably do” reads as hesitation, and it is the same second of thinking.
