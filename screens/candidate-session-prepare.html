    Quiet space & accessibility

    Find somewhere you can talk out loud for the full session without being interrupted. The interviewer waits when it hears silence, so a pause to think costs you nothing.

    Your answers are captured on your device and uploaded a few seconds behind you, so a short drop loses nothing. If the connection goes for more than 20 seconds the interview pauses and shows a reconnect screen; you have 10 minutes to come back and resume from the same question. Close the tab by accident and the same thing happens — reopen the session from your dashboard.

    If you cannot get back, the session is saved as ended early with whatever you had answered. In practice mode you can simply run it again.For a screening, contact the employer's hiring team — they can reissue the invitation.

    The interview screen is operable by keyboard alone, works with screen readers, and captions can be resized from your settings. If you need longer answer windows, a text-only interview or a break part-way through, set it up in advance — adjustments are applied to the session, not requested mid-interview.
