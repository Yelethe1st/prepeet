Why there is no score here

A screening evaluation is produced for the hiring team, against their rubric, to support a decision they make themselves. It is a set of evidence and confidence statements — not a verdict, and not something Prepeet is entitled to hand back as a personal result.

If we showed you a number you would reasonably read it as your outcome. It is not: the employer weighs it alongside your application, your references and everything else they know. So this screen stays empty for screening sessions by design, in every mode and at every point in the process.

What you can always see is what was captured, when it was delivered, and a copy of your own data on request.
