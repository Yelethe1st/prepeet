Why this page is empty for you

The evaluation for a screening interview is written for the hiring team. It contains evidence they have to weigh against their own rubric and their own shortlist, and it is deliberately not a verdict — Prepeet does not decide who is hired.

Showing you a number, a band or a per-answer critique would misrepresent a decision that belongs to the employer, so we do not build that view at all. This is a product boundary, not a permissions delay: there is nothing here that will unlock later.
