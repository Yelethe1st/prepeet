Using your practice data to improve Prepeet

Off unless you turn it on. We will not ask you again.

Off

In plain English: if you turn this on, a small number of your practice transcripts may be read by Prepeet staff, and used to check whether our evaluations are fair and accurate — for example, whether we score the same answer the same way for two different people.

What that means concretely. Transcripts are stripped of your name, employer names and any other identifying detail before anyone reads them. Audio is never used. Screening interviews are never used. Your data is not sold, not shared with employers, and not used to advertise to you.

What you get out of it. Nothing directly, and your own scores do not change either way. This is not a trade — leaving it off costs you nothing and we will not nag you.

You can turn it off later. Turning it off stops future use immediately and removes your transcripts from the review pool within 30 days.

Our accessibility commitment

Prepeet targets WCAG 2.2 AA across the candidate experience, including the live interview screen. Every chart has a text summary and a data table. Captions are available in every interview. Nothing is communicated by colour alone. If you find something that falls short, it is a bug and we will fix it.
