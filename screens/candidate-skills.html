How a competency gets a number

  1. 1 · Your answer is transcribed
    Word for word, including the pauses and the corrections. The transcript is the source — nothing is inferred from your voice, face or manner.
  2. 2 · Spans are marked against the rubric
    A span is a specific stretch of what you said that supports, contradicts or leaves a gap in a competency. Every span is quotable and shown to you.
  3. 3 · Spans combine into a score and a range
    More independent spans from more sessions means a narrower range. Fewer than three spans means no number at all.
  4. 4 · You decide what to practise next
    This is a practice signal about your answers, not a prediction of how any interview or job will go. Your data settings control how long it is kept.
