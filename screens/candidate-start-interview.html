How personas work

A persona is an interview style — pacing, how hard the follow-ups push, how much silence you get. It is never a judgement about you, and it does not describe what kind of candidate we think you are.

  • The same rubric and the same evidence standard apply whichever persona you pick.
  • Choosing Marcus will not lower your score, and choosing Ama will not raise it.
  • Samples are synthesised voices reading a scripted line. No recordings of real interviewers are used.

Why it is locked

If candidates could pick their own questions, interviewer or length, the hiring team would be comparing different interviews. A locked set-up is the only way the evidence stays comparable — and it protects you as much as it protects them.
