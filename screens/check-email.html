If it has not arrived

Most messages land in under 30 seconds. Anything slower is usually a receiving mail server queuing us. Search your mailbox for prepeet rather than scrolling — filtered mail often skips the inbox entirely.
Clinical and public-sector mail gateways frequently quarantine first-time senders and rewrite links. Ask your IT team to allow no-reply@prepeet.example and the domain prepeet.example, or use a personal address for your candidate account — you can change it later in settings.
Links last 60 minutes and stop working after one use. Some mail scanners open links before you do, which uses the link up. If that happens you will land on the expired page — request a fresh one from there.
