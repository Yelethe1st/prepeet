If it keeps happening

  1. Retry once. Most failures of this shape are transient and clear within a minute.
  2. Check the status page — if there is an open incident, the fix is already in progress and there is nothing to report.
  3. If the status page is clear and it fails a third time, contact support with the incident reference above. Include what you were doing and roughly when.
  4. If you are mid-screening and running out of time, say so in the subject line — those are triaged first, and the deadline is extended either way.

Typical first response: under two hours on a working day. Screening-blocking issues are picked up sooner.
