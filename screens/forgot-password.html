Reset your password

Tell us the address on your account. We will email a single-use link that lets you set a new password — it works for 60 minutes and only once.

Use the address you sign in with, not a forwarding alias.

Enter a valid email address so we know where to send the link.

What happens after you press that button

  1. We send one email
    From no-reply@prepeet.example, subject “Set a new Prepeet password”. It arrives within a minute in almost every case.
  2. You open the link within 60 minutes
    The link is tied to your address and stops working the moment it is used, or when the hour is up — whichever comes first.
  3. You choose a new password
    Setting a new password signs out every other device, including any interview you left running elsewhere.
Signed up with Google, Microsoft or Okta?

Then you have no Prepeet password to reset — your provider holds it. Go back and use the single sign-on button instead.
