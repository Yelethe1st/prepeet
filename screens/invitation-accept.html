Lena will chair the panel

Formal and measured. She runs a competency-based panel format with scripted follow-ups and a time box per question, the way clinical panels normally work. She will tell you when she is moving on.

How it runs

  1. Set-up and microphone check
    2 minutes
    You test your microphone, choose captions if you want them, and read the questions' subject areas in advance.
  2. Eight competency questions
    About 35 minutes
    Clinical reasoning, patient safety, escalation and handover, and compassionate communication. Lena asks follow-ups when an answer is thin.
  3. You submit
    Immediate
    Nothing is sent until you press submit. You can end early, but an unfinished interview reaches the hiring team as an unfinished interview.
  4. Northwind Health reviews it
    Usually within ten working days
    A named reviewer reads your transcript with the evaluation beside it, and contacts you directly with the outcome.
Need extra time, captions, a screen reader-friendly flow or a text-only alternative? Set it up before you start in accessibility settings. Adjustments are never shared with the employer.

Exactly what Northwind Health System will and will not see

Read this before you consent. It is the whole of what leaves your account.

They receive

  • The audio recording and a full transcript of your answers.
  • An evaluation that quotes what you actually said against each competency in their rubric.
  • How much evidence each competency rests on — including where it says insufficient evidence.
  • The eight questions asked, the follow-ups, and how long the interview ran.

They never receive

  • Any reading of your personality, mood, accent or confidence. Prepeet does not measure these and produces no such signal.
  • A hire or reject recommendation. A named reviewer at Northwind decides; Prepeet does not.
  • Anything from your practice sessions — those stay private to you, always.
  • Your other applications, or screenings you have done for other employers.
You will not be shown a score for this interview.

Screening evaluations are written for the hiring team, and a number stripped of that context would misrepresent a decision Prepeet does not make. You will see confirmation that it was submitted, the questions you were asked, and your own recording. If you want scores and coaching, run the same format in practice mode — nothing from practice reaches an employer.
