Prototype index

Every screen in the Prepeet prototype

Prepeet is a multi-tenant, voice-based AI interview platform with two modes — practice for candidates preparing, and screen for recruiter-run assessment. Every card below opens a real, working screen with seeded data, both themes and its interaction states.

How to drive it. Every app screen has a theme toggle; the candidate topbar has a Practice / Screen mock-data switch; the admin topbar has a Viewing as role switcher that changes which navigation items exist. Most data screens carry a Preview state control for loading, empty and error states. Dynamic routes use stable IDs such as ses_7Kq2XA and cand_R4T9MP.

The core journey

Interview lifecycle, end to end

Walk it in order. The last step forks on mode: a practice candidate gets coaching and evidence; a screening candidate gets a submission confirmation and nothing else.

  1. Step 1

    Configure session

    Five-step wizard: role and focus, interview shape, persona, length and difficulty, review.

    /candidate/start-interview
  2. Step 2

    Prepare & device checks

    Brief, persona preview, microphone, speaker, connection and browser checks, saved-progress notice.

    /candidate/session/[id]/prepare
  3. Step 3

    Live interview

    Phase, persona, connection, mic, waveform, speaking indicators, timer, reconnection, captions, push-to-talk.

    /candidate/session/[id]
  4. Step 4

    Process & evaluate

    Upload, transcribe, extract evidence, evaluate, prepare feedback — plus failure and delayed states.

    /candidate/session/[id]/complete
  5. Practice

    Outcome & evidence

    Score, competencies with confidence, evidence spans, transcript and audio replay.

    /candidate/session/[id]/results
  6. Practice

    Coaching review

    Per-turn evaluation, what to change, model rewrites, redo a question, drills.

    /candidate/session/[id]/review
  7. Practice

    Delivery

    Measured pace, pauses and fillers, ten delivery dimensions, drills, redo next to the original.

    /candidate/session/[id]/articulation
  8. Screen

    Submitted — no score

    The entire screening outcome a candidate sees. No score, band, chart, coaching or recommendation.

    ?mode=screen
  9. Screen

    Recruiter receives

    The same session as the recruiter sees it: evidence, coverage, contradictions, decision, audit.

    /admin/recruiter/[id]
